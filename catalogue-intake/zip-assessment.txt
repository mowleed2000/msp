Verdict
-------
Not confident to build the full Gemini CSV from this zip.

Confident to:
  - skip every CSV row with no image
  - collapse flavour SKUs onto one range card
  - add photographed phones / official Apple-Samsung / salt ranges
  - skip the clutter accessories even if the photo is in the zip

Not confident to:
  - put Vuse / Lost Mary kits / Hayati / Xros / Caliburn on the site
    from this zip (they are not in it)
  - use these thumbnails as final hero images without a quality pass

To build a good catalogue next, either:
  1. Accept this zip as phones + salts + chosen accessories only, or
  2. Add the missing vape kit pack shots (the tonight keep-list kits),
